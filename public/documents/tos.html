1. What Clean Ops is

Clean Ops is a free, community-made modification for Call of Duty: Black Ops III multiplayer (Steam). It removes known cheaters from matches, fixes exploits and adds stability, privacy and quality-of-life features. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Activision, Treyarch or Valve.

To work, the Patch connects to the Clean Ops server every time you play. How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

3. Rules

Bannable - leads to the Clean Ops blacklist:

  • using cheat software (e.g. aimbot, wallhack, spinbot, no-recoil);
  • using game-breaking exploits, including "god mode" classes;
  • deliberately crashing matches or kicking other players with exploits;
  • evading a ban - playing with another account, or letting a blacklisted player use your account or game licence (Steam Family Sharing). Accounts linked to a blacklisted account can be reviewed and blacklisted too.

Not bannable - discouraged, but not policed by Clean Ops:

  • the in-game "Pick-10 glitch" for modded classes;
  • toxic chat, trash-talk, camping or "annoying" weapons. Use the in-game mute or switch lobbies.

Also not allowed:

  • tampering with the Patch or its connection to our server, or attacking the server;
  • misusing the report system: reports are for cheating only. Players who send false or abusive reports can be warned or excluded from reporting.

Reverse engineering or decompiling the Patch is not allowed, except where mandatory law permits it.

4. Bans and appeals

Bans are decided by our moderators after reviewing reports and evidence. A banned (blacklisted) player:

  • is removed automatically from any match in which a Clean Ops user is present - whether or not the banned player uses Clean Ops;
  • can't use Clean Ops.

A Clean Ops ban doesn't affect your Steam account, your game licence, or matches in which no Clean Ops user is present.

Appeal: if you believe you were banned by mistake, open a "Ban Appeal" ticket on the Clean Ops Discord server with your Steam profile link or Steam ID64 and a short, polite explanation. A moderator reviews it. Threats or harassment end the appeal.

5. Connection, Steam check and updates

  • Clean Ops needs a working connection to the Clean Ops server. Without it, Clean Ops and online play with it may not be available.
  • Your Steam account is confirmed with Steam when you connect (Privacy Policy, section 3.2).
  • Automatic updates: at game start the Patch checks for a newer version and installs it automatically. Running an outdated version may not be possible.

6. Reports

You can report suspected cheaters in-game, while in the same match. Reports go to the Clean Ops team for review; there is no guarantee that a report leads to a ban. Reports for anything other than cheating aren't acted on.

7. P2P hosting

As with any P2P match in Black Ops III, players who join your match connect to your IP address. When you host with Clean Ops, the address is also shared with Clean Ops users so their game can recognise your session as a genuine Clean Ops session (Privacy Policy, section 3.5). We may turn P2P hosting off at any time.

8. Availability and changes

Clean Ops is a free hobby project. We don't guarantee that the Patch, the server or any feature is available at all times or works with every game or Steam update. We may change, suspend or end the Patch or any feature at any time.

9. Things to know

  • Antivirus warnings: some antivirus programs flag d3d11.dll because of how game patches work (hooking the game, downloading updates, code obfuscation and virtualisation). You may need to add an exception.
  • Compatibility: Clean Ops doesn't work together with t7patch or similar mods; running them together can cause crashes.
  • Disable / uninstall: hold Shift while the game starts to skip Clean Ops for one session; use "Delete Clean Ops" in the settings, or delete d3d11.dll from the game folder, to remove it.
